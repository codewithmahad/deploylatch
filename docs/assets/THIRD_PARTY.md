# Artwork and source notes

## Project logo and demonstration

`deploylatch-logo-original.png` is the creator-supplied DeployLatch logo, preserved unchanged. `deploylatch-mark.svg` and `deploylatch-mark-on-dark.svg` are vector adaptations of its lock, server, upload arrow, and circular recovery arrows. They use the README's cobalt-blue palette, with dark or silver foregrounds. Both banners incorporate the same adapted symbol and two-tone project name.

`deployment-demo.png` is the creator's original Ubuntu terminal screenshot, copied unchanged. It shows the successful v2 update, failed v3 deployment, recovery to v2, service status, and deployment history.

## Tux

`linux-tux.png` is the same unmodified Tux PNG used in [LINUX_LAB](https://github.com/codewithmahad/LINUX_LAB/blob/main/assets/tux.png). The image is used directly without filters, outlines, shadows, rotation, or pixel changes.

Tux was created by **Larry Ewing <lewing@isc.tamu.edu> using The GIMP**. Original artwork and reuse information: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tux.png). Its original attribution and reuse terms apply; the image is not covered by this project's code license.

## Department emblem

`ubit-emblem.jpg` is copied without modification from the maintainer's local `ubit-semester-result-dashboard/public/ubit-logo.jpg`. It depicts the Department of Computer Science, UBIT, University of Karachi. The original JPEG has a white background.

`ubit-emblem.png` is a transparent cutout prepared from that JPEG with the built-in image editing tool for README presentation. The original is retained alongside it. The emblem identifies the course institution.

Edit prompt: "Make only the plain white background outside the outer circular maroon rim fully transparent. Preserve the entire circular seal, including its internal white areas, lettering, black band, maroon rim, building, graduation cap, and colors. Do not add borders or shadows."
